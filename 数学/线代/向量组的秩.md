## 向量组的秩
**1. 定义**　向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$的最大无关组所包含的向量的个数,称为该向量组的秩,记为$R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)$.
**2. 结论**
1. 若$R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)=r$,说明$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$中最多有$r$个向量线性无关,则向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$中任意$r+1$个向量必定线性相关.
2. 若$R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)=r$,则向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$中任意$r$个线性无关的向量都可组成该向量组的一个最大无关组.
3. 向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$线性无关$\Leftrightarrow R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)=s$.
4. 向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$线性相关$\Leftrightarrow R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)<s$.
5. 仅含零向量的向量组秩为0.
6. $R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)\leqslant R(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s,\boldsymbol\beta_1,\boldsymbol\beta_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\beta_t)$.
7. 任一矩阵的秩既等于其行向量组的秩,也等于其列向量组的秩.
8. 向量组$\boldsymbol\beta_1,\boldsymbol\beta_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\beta_r$可由向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$线性表示,当$r>s$时,必有$\boldsymbol\beta_1,\boldsymbol\beta_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\beta_r$线性相关(以少表多,多的相关).
**推论**　若$\boldsymbol\beta_1,\boldsymbol\beta_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\beta_r$线性无关,且$\boldsymbol\beta_1,\boldsymbol\beta_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\beta_r$可由$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$线性表示,则$r\leqslant s$.

**定理**:设矩阵$\boldsymbol A_{m\times n}\xrightarrow{\text{初等行变换}}\boldsymbol B_{m\times n}$,则$\boldsymbol A$与$\boldsymbol B$的列向量之间有相同的线性关系.
由上述定理,可用初等变换法求向量组$\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s$的秩和最大无关组:
**Step1.** 将$\boldsymbol A=(\boldsymbol\alpha_1,\boldsymbol\alpha_2,\cdots,\boldsymbol\alpha_s)$作初等行变换得行阶梯矩阵$\boldsymbol B$.其非零行数$r$即为$\boldsymbol A$的秩.
**Step2.** $\boldsymbol B$的主元所在的$r$个列向量是线性无关的,对应矩阵$\boldsymbol A$的$r$个列向量也是线性无关的,所以$\boldsymbol B$矩阵每个主元对应的$\boldsymbol A$的$r$个列向量即为$\boldsymbol A$的一个最大线性无关组.
